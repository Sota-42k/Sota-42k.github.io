- Developed an identifiability theory for diffusion models, proving that forward noising cannot create latent identifiability and that identifiability from conditioning is preserved
- Derived an explicit, parameter-free readout of latent sufficient statistics from conditional score contrasts, with quantitative recovery guarantees linking representation error to the score-matching objective
- Extended the theory to discrete diffusion and auxiliary-variable-free settings, establishing exact recovery below an explicit error threshold in the discrete case
