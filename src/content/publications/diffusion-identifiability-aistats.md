Establishes an identifiability theory for diffusion models, proving that forward noising
cannot create latent identifiability and that identifiability from conditioning is preserved.
Derives an explicit, parameter-free readout of latent sufficient statistics from conditional
score contrasts, with quantitative recovery guarantees linking representation error to the
score-matching objective.
