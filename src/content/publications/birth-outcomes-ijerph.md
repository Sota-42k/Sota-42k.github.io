title: "Birth Outcomes in the Hispanic Population in the United States: Trends, Variation, and Determinants (2011–2021)"
authors: ["Yang, Y.", "Fujii, S.", "Nguyen, S."]
year: 2025
venue: "International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health, 22(9), 1325"
status: "peer-reviewed"
featured: true
---

Analyzes 2011–2021 United States birth records to study C-section delivery, low birthweight,
and prematurity across Hispanic-origin groups, combining large-scale data preprocessing of
over 30 million individual records with logistic regression and classification trees.
